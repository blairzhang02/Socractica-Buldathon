Making Dough — Colour Theme

Sampled directly from the Socratica × Ramp buildathon bakery artwork. Every hex below was pulled from the pixels of that image, not approximated by eye. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Chocolate — the structural colour

The stall frame, awning and hand-lettering. This is the backbone of the theme: use it for text, headers, nav bars and borders. Dark enough to carry body copy at AAA contrast on any cream.

Cream — surfaces

The paper background and the signboard. These are your page and card backgrounds. Never put cream text on cream.

Pastry case — accents

The sweets, the cards, the cherries. Bright, warm and saturated. These are fills and highlights — chart series, tags, badges, illustrations — not text colours. See the contrast table below.

Text-safe accent inks

Darkened versions of each accent, tuned until they clear WCAG AA (4.5:1) on cream. Use these whenever an accent needs to be readable rather than decorative.
